Why this matters

Citizens increasingly reach public services through AI assistants — asking a question, being guided through a procedure, or sending an agent to do it. A service that people can use but AI cannot reach, read, or operate is invisible on that route. This report asks the citizen's five questions of one service and names the cheapest fix for each gap found.

What this report can and cannot promise. LAS reports bounded page and retrieval properties; it does not test whether an AI can complete the service. Whether a given engine does retrieve the site is the engine's choice — findings hold to the extent it does. Outcomes in the AI ecosystem are tracked separately, per-engine and date-stamped (appendix A), and are never averaged into the site's results.

Pillars

Each check is tagged with the retrieval mode its fix protects — the channel content takes to reach an AI: parametric memory (what the model already knows from training; measured in appendix A), fetching (reading the raw page, no scripts), rendered DOM (reading the page after a headless browser runs its scripts), and agentic (an agent acting on the site). Two more tags: emerging interface (agent-native API access — the fifth channel) and cross-mode safety (payload hygiene every channel inherits). Tags are organizational — nothing is scored by them.

Appendix B — methodology & provenance

Run residential-1789375145 · vantage: residential, Lisbon · method: scan-service-2026-09-13-phase2 · obstacle set: 2026-09-13.2 · grading: D 2026-09-13.1, L 2026-09-13.1 · 14 Sep 2026
Scope. Designed for government service pages; any public URL may be submitted, subject to scan safety checks.
Bands and colours. A pillar of scored checks shows a descriptive band over the mean of its versioned required checks only when all required checks were measured — Strong mean ≥ 0.75 · Good mean ≥ 0.6 · Partial mean ≥ 0.4 · Weak mean ≥ 0.25 · Blocked mean ≥ 0.0 — and each band maps to one of four colours. The O pillar has no band: its detectors carry states, not numbers, and its colour is the worst severity among exact detector versions with a matching validation record that found something (red · orange · yellow). Grey is not one of the four; it means the assessment is incomplete or not measured. Nothing is added up — there is no overall score, because the pillars are not commensurable and no defensible weighting between them exists.
Scored-pillar coverage contracts. A missing required check, duplicate id, changed membership or incompatible version withholds the band rather than changing its denominator. Checks outside the required set are supplementary and never enter the mean.
D · 2026-09-13.1Required: D1. retrieval reachability from the recorded network vantage.
L · 2026-09-13.1Required: L3, L4, L5, L7. machine-readable legibility of the assessed page.
Detector states and coverage. Detected means the recorded evidence meets the detector's exact claim. Clear means the required inspection completed and the construction was not detected within that scope. Does not apply requires positive evidence and a reason. Not measured means required evidence is missing or inconclusive. A required scored check that could not run is reported as not measured and withholds the whole band; a detector that could not run reads not measured, never clear. A zero is a measured failure with a fix; an unmeasured is an honest gap.
Obstacle detectors and their validation status. A detector contributes to the O pillar's colour only after it has passed a held-out test on labelled real websites, with the acceptance level set in writing beforehand by the method owner. Until then its result appears as an observation with its evidence and colours nothing. The synthetic study pages are regression fixtures, not validation.

What the O pillar does not claim. Candidate checks report only the bounded properties in their evidence. A scan may cite the controlled study only after the check establishes the same construction and that exact detector version has a matching held-out validation record. A clean result means no obstacle was detected within the completed assessment set — not that an AI agent can complete this service.
Vantage. Reachability is scored from the vantage named in the provenance line; vantage is part of the measurement and is never mixed within a run.
Passive measurement. No form was submitted and no sign-in was attempted. The one interaction is pressing a page's own "show details" controls, behind a guard that aborts any request that is not a plain read; if a control attempts one, the step stops and reports itself unmeasured.
