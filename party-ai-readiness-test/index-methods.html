How this check was done

We audited the saved data and category rules in party-web-ai-readiness at its 13 September 2026 snapshot. We then selected twelve addresses for variation—readable candidate pages, sparse responses, errors, PDF links and party articles—and scanned them from a residential connection in Lisbon on 14 September.

Each address produced a full LAS report and a smaller structural report from the same run: 12 scans and 24 reports. These were automated website checks, with no AI model calls. Ten requested pages returned HTTP 200, one returned 403 and one returned 404. Completion means a report was produced, not that every check succeeded.

Counts describe the selected page region, not necessarily the whole page. Click tests block requests that could change state; nine stopped during page loading, and two could not assess error pages. Named-crawler probes used user-agent labels from our connection, not verified vendor infrastructure. PDF samples covered three pages per file. The full LAS reports retain government-service wording and scores, which do not judge campaign information quality.
